# The Connected Journey from Plan to Closure

## Why the journey matters

An enterprise order becomes useful to the factory only when people can connect it to a suitable
process version, resource, material and team. Its completion becomes useful to the enterprise only
when quantities, quality, consumption and exceptions can be reconciled. Andromeda connects those
parts of the journey while keeping enterprise planning, manufacturing operations and physical
control responsibilities distinct. [S01]

The following narrative describes the product relationship between activities. Plants may add
local stages and approvals; it is not a delivery plan or a fixed operating procedure.

## Define the work and relate it to demand

Engineering releases the product revision, bill of materials or formula, routing, recipe,
parameters, instructions, quality limits and eligible resources. An order then expresses the
required product, quantity, due date, priority and origin, whether received from enterprise resource
planning (ERP) or entered by a planner. The product relates the order to valid master information
and its agreed state. Repeated receipt of the same enterprise information must not create a second
manufacturing commitment.

Planning and dispatch consider capacity, calendars, concrete shift occurrences, material, tools,
maintenance and competency. This produces an understandable queue rather than an unexplained
priority number. A supervisor can change priorities within policy, with the reason and operational
impact retained alongside the earlier plan.

## Prepare and execute with the right context

Preparation relates the assigned resource to cleaning or changeover, material reservation, the
applicable process version, training and safety or quality prerequisites. A discrepancy remains
visible and follows the appropriate restriction or formal authorization; it is not removed through
an interface shortcut.

The operator sees the task, equipment state, parameters, instructions and permitted actions in the
HMI. Human reports and equipment observations contribute start, good quantity, loss, consumption,
downtime and operating conditions. Those facts stay related to order, operation, resource and shift.
Crossing midnight alone does not end an operation or reassign it to another shift.

Execution is anchored to an effective process definition, an identified physical scope and a
responsible operational owner. Without that context, later quantity and quality information cannot
reliably explain which work it belongs to or who was accountable for it.

Pauses need a recognizable cause and responsibility for resumption. A machine stoppage, material
shortage, quality hold, safety restriction and staffing issue have different consequences even
when they all interrupt production. A later classification explains the observed state without
erasing it.

## Make quality decisions and reconcile completion

Inspection can be prompted by a plan, a result or an operational event. Affected material or
production stays in the relevant quality state until an authorized decision. A nonconformity
remains connected to its order, operation, lot, asset, people and governing version.

Closure brings together good output, loss, scrap, rework, yield, consumption, leftovers, lot status
and outstanding work. These quantities need an explainable relationship, rather than a completion
flag that hides differences. Confirmation to the ERP retains the source order, reported results,
completion state and identifiable completion occurrence or version and time. Repeated confirmation
does not double the reported production; failures remain visible as reconciliation exceptions.

## Learn without rewriting the evidence

The connected record supports genealogy, indicators and corrective action. A late or duplicated
observation can change the current interpretation of performance, but remains distinguishable from
a newly occurring fact. The original shift occurrence, calendar or policy version and receipt
information explain the adjustment. Investigation and improvement preserve the evidence on which
previous decisions were made.

## Business contribution

People spend less effort reconstructing the same order across separate records. Supervisors can
explain why work was dispatched or delayed, quality can understand affected production, and the
enterprise receives results tied to its original commitment. The value depends on continuity across
the journey; isolated activity records leave reconciliation and responsibility gaps.
