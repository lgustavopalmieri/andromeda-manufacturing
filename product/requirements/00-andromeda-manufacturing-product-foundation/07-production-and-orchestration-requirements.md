# Production, Process Engineering and Operational Coordination

## The problem and intended value

An order is difficult to execute consistently when its recipe, instructions, resource eligibility
or material expectations are unclear. Local workarounds may keep production moving while making
quality, output and losses hard to explain. Andromeda connects the definition of the work with
planning, preparation, execution and the resulting evidence.

The business purpose is reproducible production with clear responsibility, supported by changes
that people can understand rather than rediscover at the machine.

## Define and govern how a product is made

The product maintains the relevant product revision, bill of materials or formula, routing,
operations, eligible resources, parameters, instructions, quality limits and controlled attachments.
For batch production, general and site recipes, recipe or procedure structure and batch records
need distinct meanings. ISA-88 provides a conceptual reference for these differences. [S02]

A process change has a configurable review, approval, effective period and communication policy.
Its consequences can reach orders, inventory, training and the HMI, so publication is more than
replacing a document. Work already started preserves the definition applied to it; a replacement
definition governs new work only under its effective and release conditions.

Reusable setup is a versioned template with declared compatibility for product, resource, location
and validity range. Reuse should reduce repeated preparation without making an unsuitable setup
appear generally applicable.

## Plan and dispatch with explainable constraints

Demand and production orders need a visible priority, assigned resources, date or shift and
sequence. Capacity, calendars, competencies, material, quality and maintenance restrictions all
contribute to whether the work is feasible. People need to understand planned capacity, actual
availability and unresolved conflicts before dispatch.

A revised queue retains the earlier plan, the person making the decision, the reason and its
operational impact. Changing a priority cannot rewrite completed work or extend a supervisor's
authority beyond the permitted resources and organizational scope.

## Follow execution and responsibility

An operation can be started, paused, resumed, closed or cancelled according to its business state.
Responsibility can also be transferred without losing who owned the work before and after the
handover. The product distinguishes good quantity, loss, scrap, rework, yield, time, consumption
and intermediate or finished output, related to order, operation, resource and shift.

Hierarchical reasons help plants describe downtime and losses consistently. Whether an explanation
is mandatory, and whether it needs dual approval, remains a tenant policy. Classification does not
silently replace the observed fact.

The electronic production record brings together the responsible operator or delegated agent,
instructions and versions, operating evidence and exceptions. Signatures or attestations carry the
meaning required by local policy rather than becoming an unexplained confirmation action.

## Operational authority

A start action may request, record or confirm a start; these are different business effects.
The HMI and the agreed equipment interaction describe the intended effect and its confirmation.
Andromeda is not the safety controller or the authority for interlocks. Clear language prevents an
operator from mistaking a request shown on a screen for confirmed physical execution. [S06]
