# Materials, Quality, Genealogy and Logistics

## The business need

A material balance does not explain whether a lot is suitable for the next operation, and a
completed order does not establish whether its output is released for shipment. Andromeda connects
identity, location, quality, consumption and disposition so people can use the right material,
contain affected production and explain where a product came from and went.

## Operational material and inventory

The product follows receipt and identification, incoming inspection, location and balance,
reservation, transfer, consumption, return, approved adjustment and cycle counting. A movement
retains origin, destination, quantity and unit, lot or serial identity, responsible party, time,
related order or process where relevant, and the reason for the change.

Material suitability includes product compatibility, revision, quality status, expiry, storage
conditions and the substitution policy. A visible balance is not authorization to consume it.
An improper use stays restricted unless the applicable policy permits a formal concession whose
reason, authority and genealogy remain visible.

## Quality decisions linked to the operation

A control plan relates a product, process, site and version to the characteristics being assessed,
method, sampling frequency or quantity, limits, instrument, instructions and consequences of a
result. Each recorded result retains its unit, method, instrument, operator or source, time,
evidence and decision. Manual and automatic observations remain distinguishable; a correction
preserves the original value and its explanation.

An out-of-limit result can lead to containment of the affected material, unit, lot or interval.
Nonconformity, rework, concession, root-cause investigation and corrective or preventive action
remain connected to genealogy, along with follow-up on whether the action addressed the problem.
An authorized person decides the relevant disposition; a hold is not cleared merely because
production continues elsewhere.

Quality authority may apply to material, a unit, lot or shipment, with configurable permissions
and segregation of duties. The evidence supports a quality management system without automatically
providing ISO certification. [S11]

## Choose the right traceability granularity

An individual unit or serial identifies one instance. A lot or batch groups a quantity produced
or received under common conditions. A logistics unit, such as a box, pallet or container,
identifies a package or load that may contain units or lots. These levels can be related but are
not interchangeable. A Global Trade Item Number (GTIN) combined with a serial or lot, and a Serial
Shipping Container Code (SSCC), illustrate these identification levels. [S09] [S16]

The required granularity depends on product, site, customer, sector, risk and practical capture.
A lot-controlled product need not invent a serial for every item; a serialized product retains
instance-level relationships. Both still need meaningful quantities, units, order and operation
context and responsible resources. The requirement does not assume universal unit serialization.

## Preserve transformations, custody and disposition

Genealogy follows consumption, production, transformation, splitting, merging, aggregation,
disaggregation, rework, relabeling, return and scrap. A corrected relationship points to its earlier
form rather than erasing it. Destination and disposition explain what happened to the material,
including whether it remains eligible for use or shipment.

For example, two input lots can be blended, part of the resulting output reworked, and produced
units packed onto a pallet. Investigation needs to follow the input-to-output transformations and
the packaging relationships in either direction. Relabeling, a return or scrapping must not make
an earlier identity disappear or make restricted stock appear available again.

A genealogy relationship retains identity and granularity, origin and destination or custody,
location, requested and actual quantity with unit, order and operation, resource, people or agent,
time, governing recipe, process and HMI versions, quality results and critical parameters where
relevant. These relationships support both operational explanation and supply-chain visibility.
EPCIS, the Electronic Product Code Information Services standard, provides a conceptual reference
for transformation and aggregation information. [S15]

## Investigation and shipping

People can investigate backward or forward from a unit, serial, lot, logistics unit, order,
asset, period, supplier or destination. Results remain within permission and commercial protection
boundaries. They describe confirmed relationships, late or missing information, estimates,
capture gaps and the period and sources covered. An incomplete record is not presented as a
complete genealogy without supporting evidence.

Packaging, labeling, weighing, picking, verification, release and dispatch connect the physical
shipment with its production and quality history. Open identification, capture and sharing
standards are preferred where they support understandable exchange. The business value is faster
containment and investigation, fewer unexplained material differences and more reliable customer
information. [S09] [S16]
